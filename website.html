    What is SABC?

STEM Assiut Biology Club, is a student-led community dedicated to making Biology easier, clearer, and more engaging. We aim to simplify complex concepts, provide clear explanations, and support students throughout their learning journey, helping them understand Biology with confidence and enjoy the process of learning.
